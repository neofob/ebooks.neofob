# Translation Agents: Common Sense (English to Vietnamese)

This project involves translating Thomas Paine's *Common Sense* from English to Vietnamese. The goal is not just
a linguistic transfer, but a cultural and emotional adaptation that preserves the original's revolutionary impact.

## 🎯 Project Goal
Translate *Common Sense* into Vietnamese while maintaining its spirit as a persuasive, egalitarian call to action. The
final text should resonate with a Vietnamese reader as a powerful political manifesto, conveying the same urgency
and conviction as the 1776 original.

## 📜 Translation Guidelines

### 1. Voice and Tone
- **Persuasive & Direct**: Use strong, active verbs and a confident tone. Avoid overly passive or hesitant language.
- **Accessible Prose**: Paine wrote for the "common people." Avoid archaic or overly academic Vietnamese that
would create a barrier between the text and the reader.
- **Revolutionary Spirit**: The language should feel bold and provocative. It must challenge the status quo and
inspire a sense of inevitable change.

### 2. Cultural and Linguistic Adaptation
- **Avoid "Translation-ese"**: Prioritize natural Vietnamese syntax and idioms over literal word-for-word
translation. If a literal translation sounds clunky or "foreign," rewrite it to be idiomatic while preserving the
exact meaning.
- **Political Concepts**: Map 18th-century British/Colonial political terms to Vietnamese equivalents that carry
the appropriate weight (e.g., concepts of "monarchy," "tyranny," and "independence").
- **Presentation**: Ensure the flow of arguments is logical and compelling within the conventions of Vietnamese
rhetorical style.

### 3. Quality Benchmarks
- **Faithfulness**: The core arguments and moral logic must remain intact. Do not soften the critique of monarchy
or the demand for independence.
- **Readability**: The text should be read as if it were originally written in Vietnamese, not as a translation
of an English text.
- **Emotional Resonance**: The reader should feel the same frustration with oppression and hope for liberation
that the original audience felt.

## 🛠 Technical Instructions
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent terminology for recurring themes (e.g., "hereditary succession," "common
sense," "natural rights").
- **Structure**: Preserve the original pamphlet's structural flow and argumentative progression.
- **Verification**: When in doubt about a specific nuance, refer to the historical context of the American Revolution
and the intended audience of the 1770s.
