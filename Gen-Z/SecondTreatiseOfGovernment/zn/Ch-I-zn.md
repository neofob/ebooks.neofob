# CHAPTER I

## AN ESSAY CONCERNING THE ACTUAL ORIGIN, SCOPE, AND GOAL OF CIVIL GOVERNMENT

**Sect. 1.** Since it's been shown in the previous discourse that:

1. Adam didn't have the level of authority over his kids, or dominion over the world, that people claim—neither
by natural right of fatherhood nor by a direct gift from God.
2. Even if he did, his heirs still had no right to it.
3. Even if his heirs did, since there's no law of nature or divine law that definitively picks the right heir for
every scenario, the succession line and who gets to rule couldn't have been definitively locked in.
4. Even if it had been locked in, knowledge of Adam's first-born line is completely gone. Across all human races
and families, nobody has a legit claim to be the eldest house or have the right of inheritance.

Since all these premises have been clearly made out, it's impossible for the rulers on earth now to get any legit
authority from what is called the "fountain of all power"—Adam’s private dominion and paternal jurisdiction.

Anyone who doesn't want people to think that government is just a result of raw power and violence—that humans
just live by the rules of the jungle where the strongest carries it, leading to perpetual chaos, riots, and rebellion
(the very things the followers of that hypothesis complain about)—must find another origin for government, another
source of political power, and another way of identifying who actually holds it, other than what Sir Robert Filmer
tried to teach us.

**Sect. 2.** For this purpose, I think it's useful to define what I mean by **political power**. The power
of a MAGISTRATE over a subject is different from that of a FATHER over his kids, a MASTER over his servant, a
HUSBAND over his wife, or a LORD over his slave. Since a single man can sometimes hold all these different roles,
distinguishing them helps us differentiate between the roles of a family father and a captain of a ship.

**Sect. 3.** **POLITICAL POWER**, then, is the authority to drop laws with the ultimate penalty (death), and
consequently all lesser penalties, for the purpose of regulating and protecting Property (the total assets: life,
liberty, and estate), and employing the community's force to execute those laws and defend the commonwealth from
foreign threats; and all of this is purely for the collective win.
