# OF NATURE IN MEN (THE INTERNAL STRUGGLE)

Nature is often hidden, sometimes beaten, but almost never fully extinguished. If you try to force it, nature just
comes back more violent. "Doctrine and discourse" (talking and studying) can make nature less annoying, but only
"custom" (habit) can actually change and subdue it.

If you want to win the battle against your own nature, don't set the bar too high or too low. If the tasks are too
big, you'll just fail and get depressed. If they're too small, you'll be a "small proceeder" even if you win every time.

At first, use "training wheels"—like swimmers using bladders. But after a while, you have to make it harder—like
dancers wearing thick shoes. If the practice is harder than the actual use, you'll reach "peak perfection."

When your nature is really strong and the win is hard, use this process:
1. **Stay and Arrest:** Stop the impulse in time (like counting to twenty-six when you're angry).
2. **Reduce Quantity:** Instead of quitting cold turkey, just do it less (like moving from "healths" of wine to
just one glass).
3. **Discontinue Altogether.**
But if you have the "fortitude" to just quit everything at once, that's the best move.

There's also an old rule: "bend nature to the contrary extreme" to set it right (as long as the extreme isn't a
vice). Don't try to force a habit with "perpetual continuance"—take breaks. The pause actually recharges the
new habit. Plus, if you're not perfect yet, practicing without breaks just means you're practicing your mistakes.

But don't trust your "victory" over your nature too much. Nature can stay buried for a long time and then suddenly
revive when the right "temptation" hits. Like Aesop's cat-girl: she sat there all demure and proper until a mouse
ran past, and then her "cat nature" took over. To avoid this, either avoid the temptation entirely or expose
yourself to it so often that you stop reacting to it.

The best way to see someone's *real* nature is:
1. **In Private:** No "performing" or "affecting" for others.
2. **In Passion:** When they're emotional, they forget their "rules."
3. **In a New Situation:** When custom can't help them.

People are happiest when their nature matches their job. Otherwise, they're just "living in a different world"
while they work. For studying, set hard hours for the stuff you *have* to do, but let the stuff you actually *like*
happen naturally—your thoughts will fly to it on their own.

Your nature is either "herbs or weeds." Your job is to water the herbs and kill the weeds.
