# OF HONOR AND REPUTATION (THE STATUS GAME)

Winning honor is just about revealing your actual worth without any "glitches." Some people try too hard to "get"
honor, but they end up being talked about without being admired. Others hide their virtue too much and get undervalued.

The pro move: do something that's never been tried, or do something that others gave up on, or do a common thing
with an "insane" level of quality. That gets you more honor than just doing a "hard" task as a follower. Also,
if you can do something that makes every different "faction" happy, you'll have a full house of supporters.

A "bad husband" of honor is someone who takes a risk that could disgrace them more than a win could honor them.

The fastest way to get honor is to "outshoot" your competitors using their own methods. Your "inner circle" (servants
and followers) also builds your rep—most of your fame "emanates from the household." To stop the "canker" of
envy, be open about your goals (focus on merit, not fame) and attribute your wins to "Divine Providence" or "luck"
rather than your own genius.

The Hierarchy of Sovereign Honor:
1. **Founders:** The GOATs who start states (Romulus, Cyrus).
2. **Lawgivers:** "Second founders" who create the rules for the long term (Solon, Justinian).
3. **Liberators:** The ones who end civil wars or free the country from tyrants (Augustus, Henry VII).
4. **Expanders:** Those who enlarge the territory or defend it honorably.
5. **Just Rulers:** Those who just make the times "good" and rule justly.

The Hierarchy of Subjects:
1. **Right Hands:** The ones the prince trusts with the heaviest loads.
2. **War Leaders:** The generals who do notable services in battle.
3. **Favorites:** The ones who are just "solace" to the sovereign—harmless but high-status.
4. **Competent Officers:** Those who execute their jobs with sufficiency.

And the highest honor of all: sacrificing yourself for the good of the country.
