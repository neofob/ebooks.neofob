# OF DEFORMITY (THE UNDERDOG FLEX)

Deformed people are often "even" with nature. Since nature did them dirty, they do nature dirty back—they're often
"void of natural affection." There's usually a link between the body and the mind; if nature glitched in one, it
might venture in the other. But humans have "willpower" (election) for their minds, while their bodies are just
"locked in" (necessity). So, discipline and virtue can override a "glitched" natural inclination.

Still, having something about your appearance that invites "contempt" acts as a perpetual spur. It forces you to find
a way to rescue yourself from the scorn. This is why many deformed people are extremely bold—first as a defense
mechanism, and then it just becomes a habit. It also makes them highly observant of other people's weaknesses,
so they have something to hit back with.

Interestingly, deformity can be a "cheat code" for rising in the world:
1. **Less Jealousy:** Superiors don't feel threatened by them, so they might treat them with more favor.
2. **Sleeping Rivals:** Competitors don't see them as a threat until they're already in power.

In the past, kings trusted eunuchs because people who are "envious of everyone" are often the most loyal to the
one person who gives them power. But they're often better as "spies" and "whisperers" than as actual magistrates.

At the end of the day, if a deformed person has "spirit," they'll seek to escape the scorn through either "virtue" or
"malice." This is why some of the most excellent people in history—like Aesop or Socrates—had physical deformities.
