# OF GARDENS (THE ULTIMATE OUTDOOR VIBE)

God started by planting a garden, and it's honestly the purest human pleasure and the best way to refresh your
spirit. Without a garden, a palace is just a "gross handywork." In the most "civilized" societies, people build
fancy houses first and then realize they need to "garden finely" later.

The Goal: A "perpetual spring" (ver perpetuum), where something is in season every single month.
- **Nov-Jan:** Evergreens (holly, ivy, juniper, cypress, fir, rosemary, lavender).
- **Jan-Feb:** Mezereon-trees, crocuses, primroses, and early tulips.
- **March:** Violets, daffodils, daisies, and almond/peach blossoms.
- **April:** Lilies, tulips, peonies, and cherry blossoms.
- **May-June:** Roses, strawberries, columbine, and apple blossoms.
- **July:** Gillyflowers, musk-roses, and early pears/plums.
- **August:** Plums, apricots, and musk-melons.
- **September:** Grapes, apples, and peaches.
- **Oct-Nov:** Medlars and late roses.
(This is for the London climate, but the vibe is the same everywhere).

The "Scent" Strategy:
Flowers smell better in the air than in your hand. The best scents are:
- **Violets:** The absolute GOAT of scents (especially the white double violet).
- **Musk-roses** and **strawberry leaves**.
- **Wall-flowers** (great for putting under a window).
- **Pinks** and **gillyflowers**.
- **Lime-tree flowers**.
- **Honeysuckles** (but keep them a bit far away).
- **Treadable scents:** Plant whole alleys of burnet, wild thyme, and water-mints so that when you walk on them,
they release their smell.

The Prince-Like Garden Layout:
At least 30 acres, divided into three parts:
1. **The Green (Entrance):** 4 acres of finely shorn grass. It has a stately hedge and "covert alleys" (covered
walkways) so you don't have to walk in the sun to get to the main garden.
2. **The Main Garden (Middle):** 12 acres. A square surrounded by a stately arched hedge on pillars. The hedge
should have little turrets with birdcages and colored glass for the sun to play on.
3. **The Heath (Exit):** 6 acres of "natural wildness." No big trees, just thickets of sweet-brier, honeysuckle,
and wild vines. Set it with "molehills" of low flowers (violets, strawberries, daisies, red roses). Some heaps
can have "standards" (small bushes) like juniper or holly on top.

Other details:
- **Alleys:** Use a variety of private, shady alleys on the sides. Some should be "sheltered" from the wind. Plant
fruit trees along the borders, but keep the flowers thin so they don't "deceive" (compete with) the trees.
- **The Mount:** In the middle of the main garden, build a 30ft hill with circular alleys and a "banqueting-house"
on top.
- **Fountains:** Fountains are a win; pools are a fail (they're just breeding grounds for flies and frogs). Use
"sprinkling" fountains or clear "bathing-pools" with paved bottoms and moving water to avoid slime.
- **Aviaries:** Only if they're big enough to have real grass and bushes, so the birds can act naturally and the
floor doesn't get foul.

Basically, a great garden is about variety and sensory pleasure, not just following a "model" or adding statues
for the sake of "magnificence."
