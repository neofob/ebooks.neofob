# OF AMBITION (THE GRIND MINDSET)

Ambition is like "choler"—it's a vibe that makes people active, earnest, and full of energy, as long as they're
moving forward. But if an ambitious person gets blocked, they become "adust"—meaning they get toxic, malignant,
and venomous. If they're rising, they're just "busy"; but if they're checked, they become secretly discontent and
start hoping for everything to go backward. That's the worst trait a servant of a state can have.

If a leader has to use ambitious people, they need to make sure they're "always progressing" and never
"retrograding." But honestly, the best move is to just not hire ambitious people at all. Because if they aren't
rising through their service, they'll just make the service fail to bring them down to their level.

However, there are a few cases where ambition is a necessity:
1. **War:** You need ambitious commanders. Taking a soldier without ambition is like "pulling off his spurs."
2. **Screens:** Ambitious people are great as "shields" for a leader. They'll take on the dangerous or envied
tasks because they're so blinded by their own rise that they don't see the risks. They're like "blind doves"
who just keep flying up.
3. **The Takedown:** Ambitious people are useful for pulling down anyone else who's "overtopping" the leader.

To keep ambitious people in check, here are some tips:
- They're less dangerous if they're from "mean birth" (not noble).
- They're less dangerous if they're "harsh" rather than "popular."
- They're less dangerous if they're "newly raised" rather than "cunning and fortified."
- **The "Favorite" Move:** Having a favorite is the best way to stop "great" ambitious people from getting too
powerful. If the "path to the boss" goes through the favorite, no one else can become over-great.
- **The "Balance" Move:** Pit ambitious people against each other so they balance out. But you need some "middle"
counselors to keep the ship steady, or it'll just roll all over the place.
- **The "Scourge" Move:** Use low-tier people as "scourges" to keep the ambitious people in line.
- **The "Confusion" Move:** If you need to take someone down but can't do it safely and suddenly, just keep
switching their favors and disgraces. Keep them in a "wood" so they never know what to expect.

There are two types of ambition: the ambition to "prevail in great things" (which is actually okay) and the
ambition to "appear in everything" (which is a disaster that ruins business). It's better to have an ambitious
person working on the "business" than an ambitious person who just wants to be the center of attention.

The goal should be to be eminent among *able* people—that's a win for the public. But trying to be the "only
figure among ciphers" (the only smart person in a room of idiots) is just a waste.

Honor has three parts: the power to do good, access to high-status people, and the ability to raise your own
fortune. A person who wants these things for the right reasons is "honest." A leader who can spot these intentions
is a "wise leader."

Basically, a state should choose ministers who care more about "duty" than "rising," and who love the work more
than the "bravery" of the position. Discern a "busy nature" from a "willing mind."
