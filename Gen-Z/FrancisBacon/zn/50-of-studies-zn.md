# OF STUDIES (THE INTELLECTUAL GLOW-UP)

Studying is for three things: delight (private vibes), ornament (social flexing), and ability (actually getting
the job done). While "experts" can handle the a-to-z of a project, the "big picture" strategy and marshalling of
affairs come from those who are learned.

The Balance:
- Too much study = sloth.
- Too much "ornament" = affectation.
- Relying only on the rules = the "scholar's humor" (being a nerd).

Studies perfect nature, and experience perfects studies. Natural abilities are like plants—they need "pruning" by
study. But studies can be too broad unless they're "bounded" by real-world experience. "Crafty" people look down on
studying; "simple" people admire it; but "wise" people *use* it. They know that the real wisdom is won by "observation."

How to Read:
Don't read just to contradict people, to believe everything blindly, or just to find "talking points." Read to
"weigh and consider."
- **Tasted books:** Read only in parts.
- **Swallowed books:** Read through, but not too carefully.
- **Chewed and digested books:** Read wholly, with diligence and attention.
(Also, some "meaner" books can be read by a "deputy" who gives you the extracts—but "distilled" books are usually
just "flashy" and lack substance).

The Results:
Reading makes you a "full" man; discussion makes you a "ready" man; writing makes you an "exact" man. If you don't
write, you need a huge memory. If you don't discuss, you need a fast wit. If you don't read, you need a lot of
"cunning" to pretend you know things.

The "Study Map":
- **Histories** $\rightarrow$ Wisdom.
- **Poets** $\rightarrow$ Wit.
- **Mathematics** $\rightarrow$ Subtlety.
- **Natural Philosophy** $\rightarrow$ Depth.
- **Moral Philosophy** $\rightarrow$ Gravity.
- **Logic & Rhetoric** $\rightarrow$ Ability to contend.

"Studies enter into manners"—meaning what you study shapes who you are. You can actually "cure" mental defects
with specific studies:
- **Wandering mind?** Study Mathematics (if you lose focus for one second, you have to start the whole proof over).
- **Can't find differences?** Study the "schoolmen" (they're the masters of "splitting hairs").
- **Can't argue a point?** Study lawyers' cases.

Every defect of the mind has a "special receipt" (a cure) in the right kind of study.
