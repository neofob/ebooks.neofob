# OF SEEMING WISE (THE FAKE-IT-TIL-YOU-MAKE-IT VIBE)

There's a take that the French are smarter than they look, and the Spaniards just look smarter than they actually
are. Regardless of how it is with nations, it's definitely a thing between people. As the apostle said about
godliness, some people have the "show" of it but none of the power—and the same goes for wisdom. There are people
who do absolutely nothing, or very little, but they do it with so much *solemnity* that they look like they're
doing something huge. It's honestly a joke, and totally fit for a satire, to see the tricks these "formalists"
use to make a surface look like it has actual depth and bulk.

Some are so closed-off and reserved that they only show their "wares" in a dim light, always acting like they're
holding something back. Even when they know they're talking about something they don't actually get, they try to
look like they're keeping secrets. Others rely on their "facial expressions" and gestures to look wise. Cicero once
roasted a guy named Piso for answering him with one eyebrow raised and the other dropped—like he was trying to
"look" like he hated cruelty.

Some think the move is to use "big words" and be super peremptory—just stating things as facts that they can't
actually back up. Others, when they hit something they don't understand, just act like it's "irrelevant" or "too
niche" to care about, trying to pass off their ignorance as superior judgment. Some people are always arguing for
the sake of it, using a tiny "subtlety" to confuse the actual point.

Generally, these types of people love being on the "negative" side of an argument. They love to object and point
out difficulties. Why? Because if you deny a proposal, the conversation ends. But if you agree, you actually have
to do some new work. This "fake wisdom" is the absolute bane of getting things done.

To wrap it up: a bankrupt merchant has a lot of tricks to pretend he's still rich, and "empty" people have even
more tricks to pretend they're sufficient. People who "seem wise" might be able to trick some people into liking
them, but *never* hire them for a real job. You're honestly better off with someone who's a bit absurd than someone
who's just a "formalist."
