# OF ANGER (THE EMOTIONAL SHORT-CIRCUIT)

Trying to totally delete anger is a "Stoic flex" that doesn't really work. The better move is: "Be angry, but
don't sin; and don't let the sun go down on your anger." You have to limit and confine it.

How to chill your natural "anger habit":
Meditate on how anger ruins your life. The best time to do this is *after* the fit is over. Seneca said anger
is like a "ruin"—it breaks itself upon whatever it falls on. If you can't stay patient, you've basically lost
possession of your own soul.

Anger is a "low-tier" emotion—it's most common in children, the elderly, and the sick. Pro tip: carry your anger
with "scorn" rather than "fear." If you act like the injury is beneath you, you stay in control.

How to stop a "fit" of anger:
Anger is triggered by three things:
1. **Feeling Hurt:** Tender people get angry more often because they feel "everything."
2. **Feeling Contempt:** When the injury is done in a way that's an insult, it "puts an edge" on the anger.
3. **Reputation:** When you feel your "rep" is being hit. The fix is to have a "thicker skin" (Telam honoris
crassiorem).

The best remedy to stop a fit? **Win time.** Make yourself believe that the time for revenge hasn't come yet,
so you can still yourself in the meantime.

How to keep anger from doing damage:
1. **Watch your words:** Avoid "extreme bitterness," especially specific, "sharp" insults.
2. **Don't leak secrets:** Revealing secrets in anger makes you "unfit for society."
3. **Don't "peremptorily" end things:** Don't cancel a deal or break a relationship in a heat of passion. Do things
that are "revocable."

How to manipulate anger in others:
- **To start it:** Find a time when they're already in a bad mood and highlight every detail that makes the injury
feel like "contempt."
- **To stop it:** 1. Pick a "good time" to tell them the news so the first impression is softer. 2. Separate the
actual injury from the "contempt"—frame it as a misunderstanding, a mistake, or just "passion."
