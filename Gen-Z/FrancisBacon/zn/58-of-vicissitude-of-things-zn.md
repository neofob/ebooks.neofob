# OF VICISSITUDE OF THINGS (THE GREAT RESET)

Solomon said, "There's nothing new under the sun." Plato thought all knowledge was just "remembering." Basically,
"novelty" is just "oblivion"—we just forget that things happened before. Everything is in a "perpetual flux."

The "Great Resets" of the world are deluges and earthquakes. They don't just kill people; they "bury" everything in
oblivion. Fire and drought just "dispeople" the land, but the other two "destroy" the history. In the West Indies,
it's more likely that a massive flood happened than an earthquake, because their rivers are huge and their mountains
are high.

Some people think that "religious wars" or the "zeal of sects" erase history (like the claims about Gregory the
Great), but that doesn't really last. The "old ways" usually come back.

In the "superior globe" (the heavens), comets have a huge effect on the world, but we just "gaze" at them instead of
studying their actual effects. There's a "toy" theory that weather patterns repeat every 35 years—the "prime." I've
checked, and there's some truth to it.

But let's talk about people. The biggest "reset" for humans is the change in religions and sects. The "true"
religion is built on a rock; the rest are just tossed around by time.

How new sects start:
1. The old religion is torn apart by "beef."
2. The leaders are "scandalous" and toxic.
3. The times are "stupid" and barbarous.
4. A "strange spirit" (a charismatic leader) comes along.
(This is exactly how Mahomet started).

A new sect only spreads if it does two things:
1. **Opposes Authority:** People love a rebellion.
2. **Gives License to Pleasure:** It makes the "voluptuous life" okay.
"Speculative" heresies (intellectual debates) don't actually change the state unless there's already a civil crisis.

How to stop new sects:
- Reform the abuses.
- Fix the small differences.
- Proceed "mildly" instead of using "bloody persecutions."
- Win over the leaders instead of enraging them.

Changes in War:
Wars change in three ways: where they happen (stages), what weapons are used, and how they're conducted. In the
past, wars moved East to West. Now, the "North" is the most "martial" region because the cold makes people "harder"
and more courageous.

When a great empire crashes, it's a disaster for the natives too, because they've become "soft" and dependent on
the empire's protection. When the "big tree" falls, everything underneath it gets crushed.

The "Evolution of War":
- **Early days:** Everything was about "numbers" and "raw valor."
- **Middle days:** Everything became about "position," "cunning diversions," and "better ordering" of the battle.

The Life Cycle of a State:
- **Youth:** Arms flourish (War).
- **Middle Age:** Learning flourishes (Philosophy).
- **Old Age:** "Mechanical arts" and "merchandise" flourish (Trade).
(Learning also has a cycle: Infancy $\rightarrow$ Youth $\rightarrow$ Strength $\rightarrow$ Old Age/Dryness).

Don't stare at the "turning wheels" of life for too long, or you'll get dizzy.
